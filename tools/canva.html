Canva

Freemium Design

An online design tool built around templates: social posts, presentations, posters, videos, and documents, made by dragging pieces into place.

What it is

An online design tool built around templates: social posts, presentations, posters, videos, and documents, made by dragging pieces into place. It runs in a web browser, with Android and iOS apps.

Pricing model

Freemium. Free, $0 for one person. The paid Pro plan is US$144 a year for one person (Individuals, checked November 2026). The background remover is paid only, and paid plans hold far more assets.

What the free plan gives you

  • Drag-and-drop editor with 1,000+ design types
  • 1.6 million+ templates
  • 4.7 million+ photos, videos, graphics, and audio
  • 5GB of cloud storage
  • 1 Brand Kit limited to 3 colours
  • Limited access to Standard and Premium AI tools
  • Export as PDF, JPG, and PNG
  • Canva Offline
  • Access to Affinity, Canva's professional design app

Free-plan limitations

  • The background remover is paid only
  • Premium elements show watermarks on your design if you use them on the free plan, so stick to free elements or your own photos
  • The Brand Kit holds only 3 colours, with no uploaded brand fonts
  • Storage stops at 5GB, and paid plans hold far more assets, including 141 million+ stock items
