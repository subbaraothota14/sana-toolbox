What it is

An AI chatbot. AI means computer programs that can read text and write text that sounds human. You type a question or a request, and it writes back a draft, an explanation, ideas, or an edit of your text.

Pricing model

Freemium. Free with an account. The strongest models and the longest memory are paid only (no prices stated in the article).

What the free plan gives you

  • Everyday text chat on the free model, unlimited since August 2026, with abuse safeguards
  • Drafting emails and essays, brainstorming ideas, and explaining hard topics
  • Web search for current information
  • Some file uploads, image generation, and voice chat

Free-plan limitations

  • The extras have their own small daily caps: file uploads, image generation, and voice chats each run out separately
  • Long documents may not fit in one message
  • The strongest models and the longest memory are paid only
  • In some countries, the free plan now shows ads
  • Your chats may be used to train the AI unless you change the data setting or use a temporary chat, so do not paste private or client-confidential text into it
