What it is

An AI chatbot built for long, careful writing: essays, reports, and detailed edits. It is made for steady, careful work rather than quick one-line answers.

Pricing model

Freemium. Free with an account, no card needed. The top model, Opus, and the deeper reasoning mode are paid only (no prices stated in the article).

What the free plan gives you

  • The Sonnet model, Claude's mid-level model: strong for writing and editing, but not the top model
  • Web search
  • File uploads
  • Projects for keeping one job's files together
  • Artifacts, a side panel that shows longer outputs like documents as a clean page

Free-plan limitations

  • Usage is metered on a rolling five-hour window, and Anthropic does not publish a fixed number
  • Most sources put it at roughly 15 to 40 short messages per window
  • Long chats eat the allowance faster because the whole conversation counts each time
  • The top model, Opus, and the deeper reasoning mode are paid only
  • When you hit the limit, you wait until the window resets
