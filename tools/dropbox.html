Dropbox

Freemium Storage

The original file-sync service: a folder on your computer that syncs to the cloud and to your other devices, with sharing links that almost everyone already knows how to open.

Pricing model

Freemium. Free, $0 for one person. The paid step jumps straight to Plus: 2 TB for $9.99 a month billed yearly ($11.99 billed monthly, checked November 2026). There is no 100 GB paid step.

What the free plan gives you

  • 2 GB of storage (Dropbox Basic)
  • Sync on Windows, Mac, and Linux
  • Sharing by link
  • 30 days of version history and file recovery, so you can restore a file you changed or deleted within the last month

Free-plan limitations

  • 2 GB is the smallest free allowance in this article — a few hundred documents, or a handful of photos, and it is full
  • Basic limits you to being signed in on up to three devices at a time
  • No 100 GB paid step, so Dropbox only makes sense once you need a lot of room or share with people who already use it

Features

Windows, Mac, and Linux.

Best use cases

  • Students and freelancers who share files with clients, classmates, or teams who already use Dropbox
  • Anyone who values reliable sync over a big free allowance

Pros

  • 30-day file recovery on the free plan

Cons
