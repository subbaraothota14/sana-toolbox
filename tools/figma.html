Figma

Freemium Design

A design tool for screens: app and website layouts, wireframes, and prototypes, built for working together in real time.

What it is

A design tool for screens: app and website layouts, wireframes, and prototypes, built for working together in real time. A wireframe is a rough layout of a screen, showing where buttons, text, and images go before the final design. A prototype is a clickable mock-up that shows how the screens connect.

Pricing model

Freemium. Free on the Starter plan. Dev Mode, which developers use to turn designs into code, and the full website publishing experience are paid. No paid prices are stated in the draft.

What the free plan gives you

  • Unlimited files in the Drafts space, shared with unlimited viewers
  • Co-editing with unlimited users on up to 3 files, with a limit of 3 pages per file
  • 500 AI credits a month
  • Templates and UI kits
  • Figma Education plan for qualifying students and educators, giving the full Professional plan free, valid for two years for K-12 and higher education

Free-plan limitations

  • Only 3 files can be co-edited, and each file holds only 3 pages, so a fourth shared project means moving or deleting something
  • Version history is limited on Starter; paid plans get unlimited version history
  • Dev Mode is paid
  • The full website publishing experience is paid
