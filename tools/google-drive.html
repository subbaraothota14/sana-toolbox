Google Drive

Freemium Storage

Google's cloud storage: files, photos, and documents saved online, synced across your devices, with Google Docs, Sheets, and Slides built in.

Pricing model

Freemium. Free, $0 for one person. The paid step is Google One Basic: 100 GB for $1.99 a month (checked November 2026). There is no smaller paid step between 15 GB and 100 GB.

What the free plan gives you

  • 15 GB of storage, shared across Google Drive, Gmail, and Google Photos
  • File sync on Windows, Mac, Android, and iOS
  • Sharing by link, and sharing a folder without sending the files themselves
  • The free web versions of Docs, Sheets, and Slides
  • Search inside your files

Free-plan limitations

  • The 15 GB is one shared pool, so a full Gmail inbox and a full photo library eat the same space as your Drive files
  • When the pool is full, Drive stops syncing and Gmail stops receiving mail

Features

  • Cloud storage with file sync across devices
  • Google Docs, Sheets, and Slides built in
  • Link and folder sharing
  • Search inside files

Supported platforms

Windows, Mac, Android, iOS, and the web.

Best use cases

  • Students and freelancers who already live in Gmail and Google Docs
  • Anyone who wants the biggest free allowance from a mainstream provider

Pros

  • 15 GB free — the biggest free allowance from a mainstream provider here
  • Docs, Sheets, and Slides built in
  • Syncs across Windows, Mac, Android, and iOS

Cons

  • 15 GB is shared with Gmail and Google Photos, so it can fill up silently
  • No paid step between 15 GB and 100 GB
