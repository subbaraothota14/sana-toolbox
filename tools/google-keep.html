Google Keep

Completely free Notes

Google's simple sticky-note app, built for fast capture.

Pricing model

Completely free with a Google account. No paid tier is mentioned in the article.

What the free plan gives you

  • Unlimited notes, synced to all your devices
  • Labels, colors, and pins
  • Reminders, voice memos, and photos

Free-plan limitations

  • There are no folders and no deep structure
  • Formatting is basic
  • Treat it as a capture tool, not a study system
  • Needs internet to sync
