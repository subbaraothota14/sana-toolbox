Google NotebookLM

Freemium Notes

Google's AI study tool. You upload your own notes, slides, and PDFs, and it answers questions using only those files.

What it is

AI means computer programs that can read text and answer questions about it. NotebookLM can also turn your files into study guides, quizzes, flashcards, and spoken audio summaries of your material.

Pricing model

Free plan with usage limits. Free with a Google account. The article refers to a plans page for current counts, but names no paid plan or price, so none is stated here.

What the free plan gives you

  • Up to 100 notebooks
  • Up to 50 sources in each notebook
  • Each source up to 500,000 words
  • Chat answers with citations back to your files
  • Study tools: summaries, flashcards, quizzes, and audio overviews

Free-plan limitations

  • Daily AI use has a budget that refills over hours, plus a weekly cap
  • Big audio and video outputs use the budget fastest
  • Google changes the exact counts, so check the plans page
  • You need internet to use it
  • Your files are processed on Google's servers, not only on your device
