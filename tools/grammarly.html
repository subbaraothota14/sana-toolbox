Grammarly

Freemium Writing

A writing checker that fixes spelling and grammar wherever you type: in your browser, in documents, and on your phone keyboard.

What it is

A writing checker that fixes spelling and grammar wherever you type. It works through a browser extension, a desktop app, and a mobile keyboard, and it also shows the tone of your writing.

Pricing model

Freemium. Free with an account. Full-sentence rewrites, plagiarism checks, and AI detection are paid only (no prices stated in the article).

What the free plan gives you

  • Spelling and grammar checks
  • The tone of your writing shown to you
  • Browser extension, desktop app, and mobile keyboard
  • 100 AI prompts a month (one prompt is one request you type to the AI, such as “make this paragraph shorter”)

Free-plan limitations

  • Full-sentence rewrites are paid only, so the free plan fixes errors but does not rewrite whole sentences for you
  • Plagiarism checks and AI detection are paid only (plagiarism means copying someone else's words or ideas without credit)
  • The 100 AI prompts run out fast if you use them every day

Features
