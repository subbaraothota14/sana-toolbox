What it is

A grammar and style checker that works in more than 30 languages, not just English. That makes it the strongest pick here if you write in more than one language.

Pricing model

Freemium. Free on the LanguageTool website. Advanced style rules, a stricter checking mode, and plagiarism detection are paid only. Paid prices differ a lot between sources, so the article gives no paid price — check the pricing page before paying.

What the free plan gives you

  • Spelling, grammar, punctuation, and basic style checks
  • More than 30 languages, including Spanish, French, German, and Portuguese
  • Checks up to 2,000 characters at a time (roughly 300 words)
  • 3 AI rephrasings a day

Free-plan limitations

  • The free plan checks up to 2,000 characters at a time, roughly 300 words, so a long essay takes many pastes
  • Only 3 AI rephrasings a day
  • Advanced style rules, a stricter checking mode, and plagiarism detection are paid only
  • Some sources say the browser extension needs a paid plan, so check that before you rely on it where you write
