MEGA

Freemium Storage

A privacy-first cloud storage service: your files are encrypted on your device before they are uploaded, so only you and the people you authorise hold the keys.

At a glance

Free plan
20 GB of free storage, the largest free allowance in this article
Platforms
Windows, Mac, Linux, iPhone, Android, web
Best for
the most free space with real encryption on the free plan

Pricing model

Freemium. Free, $0 for one person. Paid plans are priced in euros, not dollars: Essential is €3.33 a month billed yearly for 200 GB (checked October–November 2026).

What the free plan gives you

  • 20 GB of free storage, the largest free allowance in this article
  • Zero-knowledge encryption on the free plan too — the provider cannot read your files, because the encryption keys stay with you
  • Sync on Windows, Mac, Linux, iPhone, Android, and the web
  • Sharing by link
  • Up to 100 versions of a file

Free-plan limitations

  • Downloads on the free plan count against a transfer quota, which is limited — MEGA does not publish the exact number, and heavy downloading can pause until the quota resets
  • If you lose your recovery key, MEGA cannot reset it for you — keep that key somewhere safe, separate from your MEGA account
  • MEGA warns that free accounts left unused for a long time can eventually be deleted, so sign in now and then if you keep files there

Features

  • Zero-knowledge encryption: files are encrypted on your device before upload
  • Sync across desktop, mobile, and web
  • Link sharing and up to 100 file versions

Best use cases

  • Students and freelancers who want the most free space and real encryption on the free plan
  • Anyone who does not need to download large files constantly
