Microsoft OneNote

Freemium Notes

Microsoft's free digital notebook, organized like a real binder.

What it is

You get notebooks, sections (like tabs in a binder), and pages. You can type anywhere on a page, draw with a pen, and paste images.

Pricing model

Freemium. Free with a free Microsoft account. The AI helper Copilot and some advanced features need a paid Microsoft 365 plan (no price stated in the article).

What the free plan gives you

  • Notebooks, sections, and pages
  • Type anywhere on a page, draw with a pen, and paste images
  • Apps for Windows, Mac, Android, iPhone, and the web
  • Notes sync through OneDrive, Microsoft's online storage
  • Apps work offline and sync when you reconnect

Free-plan limitations

  • The AI helper Copilot and some advanced features need a paid Microsoft 365 plan
  • Your notes live in OneDrive, so your free OneDrive storage applies

Features

  • Binder-style organization: notebooks, sections, pages
  • Free-form typing anywhere on a page
  • Pen drawing and pasted images
  • OneDrive sync, with offline apps that sync on reconnect

Supported platforms

Windows, Mac (macOS), Android, iPhone (iOS), and the web — exactly as stated in the article.

Best use cases

  • Students who already use Microsoft tools
  • Students who like a binder-style notebook, including handwritten notes with a pen
