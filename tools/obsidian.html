Obsidian

Free forever Notes

A notes app that stores your notes as simple text files on your own device.

What it is

It works without internet, and you do not need an account for personal use.

Pricing model

Free forever for personal use: the full app is free. Two paid extras exist separately: official Sync costs from $4 per month billed yearly ($5 month to month), and publishing notes as a website costs extra (no price stated in the article).

What the free plan gives you

  • The full app, free for personal use
  • Unlimited notes and all core features
  • Link notes to each other
  • Community plugins (a plugin is a small extra tool made by other users, for example a calendar or a flashcard tool)

Free-plan limitations

  • Syncing between devices is not included (sync means keeping the same notes on all your devices)
  • Official Sync costs from $4 per month billed yearly ($5 month to month)
  • You can also sync yourself with a cloud folder such as Dropbox or iCloud
  • Publishing notes as a website costs extra
  • Setup takes more time than the other apps here; it is easy to spend hours customizing instead of studying
