pCloud

Freemium Storage

A Swiss-based cloud storage service with a desktop app that works like a virtual drive on your computer, and a one-time lifetime plan instead of a monthly subscription.

Pricing model

Freemium. Free, $0 for one person. The paid step is Premium: 500 GB for $4.99 a month or $49.99 a year (checked November 2026). Lifetime plans, checked November 5, 2026: 500 GB for $219 once (listed at $279) and 2 TB for $499 once (listed at $709). Those are the discounted prices pCloud normally shows; sale prices can drop lower, so check the pricing page on the day you buy.

What the free plan gives you

  • Up to 10 GB of free storage after completing the account setup steps
  • Desktop and mobile apps, with a virtual drive, so you can open files without downloading them first
  • File versioning

Free-plan limitations

  • The free plan keeps deleted files and earlier versions for 15 days only (30 days on paid plans), so a file you delete and notice a month later is gone
  • Client-side encryption is a paid add-on, called pCloud Crypto, not included by default — without it, pCloud can technically access your files
  • Lifetime prices change with promotions — check the official pricing page on the day you buy, and do not buy a lifetime plan for storage you do not yet need
