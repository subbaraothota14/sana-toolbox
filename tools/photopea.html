Photopea

Freemium Design

A Photoshop-style photo editor that runs entirely in your browser: layers, masks, and full PSD files, with nothing to install.

What it is

A Photoshop-style photo editor that runs entirely in your browser: layers, masks, and full PSD files, with nothing to install. PSD is Photoshop's file format. Layers let you stack edits like transparent sheets, so you can change one part without touching the rest. A mask hides part of a layer without deleting it.

Pricing model

Freemium. Free, with no account needed. A paid Premium tier removes the ads. No Premium price is stated in the draft.

What the free plan gives you

  • The full editor: layers, masks, layer styles, smart objects, adjustment layers, channels, and paths
  • Cropping, resizing, filters, and vector graphics
  • Opens and saves PSD files
  • Opens RAW photo formats such as DNG, CR2, NEF, and ARW
  • Your files are processed on your own device and never uploaded
  • Exports carry no watermark from the editor
  • No account needed: open the website and start editing

Free-plan limitations

  • The free tier shows ads beside the canvas
  • AI features, such as one-click background removal, are limited on the free tier
  • Large or complicated projects can slow down, because everything runs on your device
  • There is no template library for quick social posts, so this is an editor, not a template tool
