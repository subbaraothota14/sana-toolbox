QuillBot

Freemium Writing

A paraphrasing tool. You paste in a sentence or paragraph, and it rewrites it in different words.

What it is

A paraphrasing tool. Paraphrasing means saying the same idea in new words. It also has a grammar checker, a summarizer that shortens long text, and a citation generator that formats sources in styles like APA and MLA.

Pricing model

Freemium. Free with an account. The other modes, such as Formal and Academic, and the plagiarism checker are paid only (no prices stated in the article).

What the free plan gives you

  • Paraphrasing up to 125 words at a time, in two modes: Standard and Fluency
  • Summarizer handling up to 1,200 words at a time
  • Translator handling up to 5,000 characters
  • Grammar checker that catches basic errors

Free-plan limitations

  • 125 words is about one short paragraph, so a full essay takes many passes
  • The meaning can drift between passes when rewording a long text piece by piece
  • The other modes, such as Formal and Academic, are paid only
  • The plagiarism checker is paid only
  • AI features have a small daily cap
